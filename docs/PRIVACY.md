Wallet is a personal expense manager made by **[YOUR NAME]** ("we"). This policy explains what the app stores
and why.

## Data stored on your device
Transactions, categories, budgets, settings and receipt photos you add are stored **on your device**.
They are not sent anywhere unless you turn on Family sync or export/share them yourself.

## Family sync (optional)
If you sign in to Family sync, we store:
- your **email address**, used only to sign you in and to show household members who is in the household;
- your household's **transactions, categories, budgets and receipt photos**, so they can be synced between
  your household members' devices.

This data is stored with our hosting provider **Supabase** (supabase.com) and protected so that only members
of your household can access it. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

## What we don't do
- No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no selling or sharing of data with third parties.
- We don't access your bank accounts. Everything in the app is entered by you.

## Permissions
- **Camera / Photos**: only to attach receipt photos you choose.
- **Face ID / fingerprint**: only for the optional app lock. Biometric data never leaves your device and is
  never seen by the app.

## Deleting your data
- Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on your device.
- In Family sync, choose **Leave household**. When the last member leaves, the household's cloud data is deleted.
- To delete your account, email **[YOUR EMAIL]** and we will delete it within 30 days.
